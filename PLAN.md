# English 120: Zero to Confident — the 120-day plan

> A 120-day course in grammar, speaking, writing, and professional English. Levels: CEFR A1 to C2. Daily commitment: 45-60 minutes.

This file is generated from `ops/curriculum.json`. Do not edit it by hand: change the JSON, then run `node scripts/build-all.mjs`.

## How the course works

Every day is one self-contained lesson built from the same five parts, so grammar, speaking, and writing improve together instead of in separate silos.

| Part | Time | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Warm-up | 5 min | Review yesterday, preview today, learn 8-10 new words |
| 2. Grammar | 15 min | One rule, explained plainly, with a table and common mistakes |
| 3. Speaking lab | 15 min | Formal and informal dialogues, pronunciation notes, role-play task |
| 4. Writing lab | 15 min | A model text, a structure you can reuse, and an editing checklist |
| 5. Practice and key | 5-10 min | Exercises with a full answer key and a self-check score |

Each lesson ends with **Tomorrow** (what comes next) and **Go deeper** (one or two curated sources from [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md)).

### Study rhythm

1. Read the lesson once without writing anything.
2. Do the exercises in a notebook or a notes app, then mark them with the key.
3. Say the speaking lab dialogues **out loud** twice: once slowly, once at natural speed.
4. Write the writing task in 10 minutes, then edit it with the checklist.
5. Add new words to your personal vocabulary log and review them on Day 7, 14, 21, and so on.

Assessment days (every 15th day) contain a longer task, a scoring rubric, and a review list. They are the natural place to repeat earlier material.
