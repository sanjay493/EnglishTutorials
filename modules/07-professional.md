# Module 7 · Professional & Business English

**Level:** B2-C1 · **Days:** 91-105 · **Written:** 0/15

> Perform in meetings, negotiations, presentations, email, and business writing, formal and informal.

## Checkpoint

You can chair a meeting, pitch an idea, handle a client, and produce a polished business document.

## How to work through this module

1. Read each lesson once, then do the exercises without looking at the key.
2. Record the speaking lab on your phone; compare it with the model dialogue.
3. Keep every writing task in one folder, even the bad ones. You will revise them on the assessment day.
4. On the last day of the module, take the checkpoint without notes, then review only what you missed.
